# reSET by BluLateral — Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 9, 2026.**

BluTapp, Inc. (“BluLateral,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) provides reSET by BluLateral (the “App”). This policy explains how we handle personal information when you use the App and its connected-device features. It is separate from the policy for the BluLateral mobile application.

## 1. Information We Handle

### Account and sign-in information

We handle your name, email address, account identifiers, sign-in and verification information, and any profile image you provide. If you use reSET through an organization, we also handle your membership, role, invitations, and access permissions. Sign-in records may include your IP address and browser or device information. We use your email address to deliver sign-in messages and respond to you.

### App content and activity

The App stores available and downloaded Reset content, preferences, acknowledgments, playback position, and when a Reset was last played. Our services process requests needed to provide the content available to your account. Some information is stored on your phone so the App can resume your experience.

### Connected devices

If you connect compatible BluLateral devices, we handle device identifiers, device names, pairing information, connection history, battery or operating status, and information needed to support device updates. We use this information to connect and operate the devices and troubleshoot problems.

Bluetooth access is needed to discover and connect nearby devices. On some older Android versions, nearby-device discovery also requires location permission; we use that permission for discovery, not GPS tracking. Camera access may be requested to scan a pairing code. You can manage these permissions in your phone's settings. Disabling a required permission can prevent the related feature from working.

### Health-related information and research

If your account has access to supported measurement features and you use compatible hardware, the App may handle physiological measurements and related sensor readings, including heart rate and blood-oxygen estimates, measurement times, and quality indicators. We treat this as sensitive health-related information. These categories describe the information handled, not the design or capabilities of any particular device model.

Enabling access to these features can automatically associate your account with a research participant record. Where research upload is available, you can initiate an upload for a selected date range. Uploaded information can include measurements and related readings associated with coded participant and device identifiers.

**Coded research information is not necessarily anonymous:** it may remain linkable to you through restricted records. Creating a participant record is not the same as obtaining consent. This privacy policy does not constitute consent to research participation. Research purposes, participating organizations, any required consent, and withdrawal conditions must be addressed in a separate study notice before participation requiring that notice or consent.

### Diagnostics and support

Some testing versions and supporting services use Sentry for limited error and crash diagnostics. These reports can include app version, technical error information, and information about the circumstances of a malfunction. Diagnostic controls are designed to exclude identifying account information, sign-in secrets, health measurements, and user-provided content.

If you contact us or submit feedback, we receive the information you choose to provide. Please avoid including passwords, sign-in codes, or unnecessary health information. Feedback sent through TestFlight or Google Play is also handled by Apple or Google under their policies.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- provide sign-in, account access, and organization-based access;
- deliver Reset content, downloads, and playback;
- connect and support compatible devices;
- provide enabled measurement features and process research uploads;
- diagnose problems, protect the service, and prevent abuse;
- respond to support and privacy requests.

Research-specific use must be described in the applicable study notice. Using the App does not, by itself, authorize unspecified research uses of your information.

## 3. Service Providers and Other Access

We use Cloudflare to support our hosted services, data storage, sign-in email delivery, and backups. Sentry processes diagnostics when enabled as described above. Apple and Google provide app-distribution and beta-feedback services. Their own privacy policies also apply to information they handle through those services.

Authorized personnel may access information as needed to operate and support the service. If you use the App through an organization or research program, authorized administrators may access information associated with that organization or program, including uploaded research records where permitted. The applicable research notice must identify the research organizations and uses; access restrictions do not make the information anonymous.

## 4. Retention

Our retention policy is to keep personal information for the purposes described in this notice, taking into account the type of information, whether your account or program remains active, support and security needs, and applicable legal requirements. Information should not be retained indefinitely merely because it can be stored.

Local App information remains on your phone until removed by App cleanup or applicable device controls. Logging out is designed to clear your local account data and downloaded content. This does not remove information already held by our services or connected devices.

Account records, research information, support correspondence, and operational records can have different retention periods. Research retention and withdrawal conditions must be explained in the applicable study notice. Copies may remain in backups or exports until their applicable retention period expires. Deletion requests may therefore involve more than the information visible in the App; contact us for the scope and timing applicable to your request.

## 5. Your Choices and Privacy Requests

You can manage device permissions, log out to clear local App data, and choose whether to initiate a research upload. These choices do not erase information already uploaded.

**To request deletion of your account or information held by us, email [info@blulateral.com](mailto:info@blulateral.com).** Include “reSET privacy request” in the subject and identify the email address associated with your account. Do not include your password, sign-in code, or health records. We may need additional information to verify your identity and understand your request.

**At present, the App's “Delete Account” action removes local App information but does not delete your hosted account or previously uploaded research information.** Uninstalling the App or logging out also does not delete those records. Please use the contact route above to request their removal.

Depending on where you live and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, receive a copy of, or request deletion of your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain uses. You can contact us to ask about those rights or raise a concern. Any legally permitted retention or identity-verification requirement may affect a request. Where applicable, you may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Research withdrawal and deletion of previously collected research information are separate requests and can have different effects. Contact us and the relevant study contact for the terms applicable to your participation. This notice does not promise immediate or automatic erasure of every copy.

## 6. Security and International Processing

We use reasonable safeguards to protect personal information and limit access. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Applicable privacy and international-transfer requirements must be addressed for the services and research programs involved.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13, or a higher minimum age where required by local law. This notice does not authorize participation by children in a research program. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can address the concern.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We will identify updates by revising the date of this policy and provide additional notice where required. Changes to this policy do not themselves establish consent to research or other uses requiring separate consent.
